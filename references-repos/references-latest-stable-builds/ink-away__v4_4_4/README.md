Ink Away is a drawing, notes and book annotation app for KOReader, with **palm rejection** and **pen pressure** for stylus users.

* **Draw** with pens of every kind, watercolor and smudge, on up to five layers.
* **Take notes** in notebooks of lined, grid, dotted or planner pages, with handwriting turned into text.
* **Annotate any book** KOReader opens, EPUB to PDF, with ink that follows its words when you change the font.
* **Take your annotations with you.** Export a book you have written on as a PDF with your ink, highlights and pictures where you put them and its text still searchable, as PNGs, or as an editable copy of the original PDF.

## Install

<a href="https://github.com/EmirErtorer/ink-away.koplugin/releases/latest"><img src="assets/readme-download.svg" alt="Download the latest release"></a>

Unzip the download and copy the `ink-away.koplugin` folder into KOReader's `plugins` directory, then restart KOReader:

- Kindle: `koreader/plugins/`
- Kobo: `.adds/koreader/plugins/`
- Android: `koreader/plugins/` in app storage

Open it from Top menu, **Tools** tab, "Ink Away (drawing canvas)". After that it can update itself: Settings, Updates.

## Start

1. **To draw or take notes, swipe up the right edge** in KOReader's file browser. File, New notebook gives you lined, grid or planner pages.
2. **To write on a book, open it and swipe down the right edge.** Write on the page, close with the X, and your ink stays with the book.
3. **Tap a tool, then tap it again** for its settings. The Guide button in Settings explains the rest, each card with "Show me".

If your reader already uses the right edge, use a two-finger swipe from the bottom right corner toward the top left (and from the top right toward the bottom left on a book). Every gesture can be changed in Settings, Gestures and pen buttons.

## What's inside

* **Pens and shapes:** fineliner, fountain, calligraphy, pencil, highlighter, marker, watercolor and smudge, shapes that snap, text boxes, pictures, symmetry and a lasso.
* **Notebooks:** any number of pages on eighteen papers and planners, PDFs opened as notebooks, links and a contents page, a library with folders, search and a 30-day trash.
* **Books:** ink anchored to the words, a smart highlighter that makes KOReader's own highlights, and a notebook for each book.
* **Export:** PNG, PDF and editable PDF. A book comes out as a fixed PDF laid out as you read it, in your colours, with selectable text. You can also send files to a phone or computer over Wi-Fi.
* **Made for e-ink:** palm rejection, stylus buttons, dark mode, colour screens, light refreshes, and Ink Away in ten languages. It can also give your notebooks and drawings their own shelves on [Bookshelf](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin).

The full list, with every setting and gesture, is in [docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md). What changed in each version is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Notes

- Palm rejection needs KOReader 2026.07+.
- Drawings and notebooks are saved automatically in an "ink away" folder. Exports go to "ink away/exports" unless you pick another folder.

## How it's made

I'm a computer science and engineering graduate, and I design and test Ink Away on my Kindle Paperwhite. Its code is written with Claude Code, an AI coding assistant, with me directing every change and about 4,000 automated checks behind it. Anything I can't test there goes out as a prerelease first.
