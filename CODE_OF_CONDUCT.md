# Code of Conduct

Panels+ is a small project and would be nice for everyone who
- reports a bug
- asks a question
- suggests a feature
- sends a fix

to feel welcome here, whatever their:
- age,
- body size
- visible or invisible disability
- ethnicity
- sex characteristics
- gender identity and expression
- level of experience
- education
- socio economic
- status
- nationality
- personal appearance
- race
- caste
- colour
- religion
or
- sexual identity and orientation.

## What I expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to KOReader or to
  GitHub.
- Assume good intent. There could be people here are writing in a language that is not
  their first.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not okay

- Insults, slurs and personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention.
- Harassment, in public or in private, including following someone around
  after they asked you to stop.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as an address or an email,
  without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a
  welcoming space.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and releases) and
anywhere you speak for the project, such as a post about Ink Away.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, write to me at emir1.ertorer@gmail.com. Every
report is read and kept private. I may remove comments, lock threads or block
people who break these rules, for a while or for good, depending on what
happened.
